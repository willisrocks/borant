  [1mThe Tang Dynasty: Tea Becomes Culture[0m

  The transformation of tea from a local remedy into a cornerstone of Chinese civilization took place during the Tang
  dynasty (618–907). As the empire grew prosperous and its canals and roads knit together distant provinces, tea moved
  northward from the south, and with it came a new set of social meanings. Buddhist monks played a crucial role. Tea
  helped them stay alert during long hours of meditation, and monasteries became centers of cultivation and preparation.
  The association between tea and contemplative practice, which would later flourish in Japan, has its roots here.
